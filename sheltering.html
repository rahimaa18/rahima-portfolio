Mountain Sheltering

Proper shelter helps protect climbers from cold, wind, snow and changing weather.

Camps & Mountain Shelters

Depending on the expedition, climbers may use tents, mountain huts or established base camps.

Shelter selection should consider weather, location, altitude, wind exposure and available resources.

Proper sleeping equipment, insulation and emergency supplies are important parts of mountain preparation.
