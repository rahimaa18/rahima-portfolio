Mountain Hazards

Mountaineering can be exciting, but there are also some dangers that every climber should know about.

Common Hazards

  • Rockfall
  • Avalanches
  • Bad Weather
  • High Altitude
  • Slippery Paths

Climbers should always prepare properly and check the weather conditions before starting their journey.

🌨️ Extreme Weather

Snowstorms, strong winds, heavy rain and rapid weather changes can create dangerous conditions.

❄️ Avalanches

Large amounts of snow can suddenly move down steep mountain slopes.

🏔️ Altitude

High altitude can affect the human body and requires proper acclimatization.

🪨 Rockfall

Loose rocks can fall from mountain walls and create serious hazards.

🌫️ Poor Visibility

Fog, snow and clouds can make navigation difficult.

🥶 Cold Exposure

Low temperatures can lead to serious cold-related injuries.
