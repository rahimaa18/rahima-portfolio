Types & Styles of Climbing

Different climbing styles require different skills, equipment and levels of experience.

🧗

Rock Climbing

Climbing natural rock formations using hands, feet and specialized climbing equipment.

❄️

Ice Climbing

Climbing frozen waterfalls, ice formations and steep icy mountain surfaces.

🏔️

Alpine Climbing

A combination of rock, snow and ice climbing in high mountain environments.

🪨

Bouldering

Short climbing routes performed without ropes, normally over protective mats.

🧗‍♂️

Sport Climbing

Climbing routes equipped with permanent protection points.

⛰️

Expedition Climbing

Long mountain expeditions requiring planning, camps and significant preparation.
